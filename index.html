My name is Libor Štefek.

I have been working in IT since 1989 – more than 35 years of hands-on experience in software development, databases and data integration. Since 1993 I have been with LOGIS a.s., a Czech company developing advanced planning and scheduling systems for manufacturing, where I work as an analyst, developer and integration consultant.

My core areas are relational databases (Oracle, SQL Server, PostgreSQL), SQL and PL/SQL, ETL and data integration, and Unix/Linux systems. I design data models and integration architectures that connect planning systems with ERP and other enterprise software, and I take care of build, deployment and automation tooling.

I have worked on international projects for customers such as Nokia, Nokia Siemens Networks and The Timken Company, and on planning system implementations in Czech industry.

I also work with LabKey Server in a healthcare environment, focusing on data migration and integration of clinical research data.

You can find more details on my LinkedIn profile. If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact me.
